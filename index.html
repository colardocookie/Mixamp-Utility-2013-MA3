Import
Your headphone measurement

Load your headphone's frequency response as a CSV with frequency,raw columns. Best is the CSV from AutoEq's results folder: it also carries AutoEq's own Harman reference for that rig, so the fits match AutoEq exactly. Pick the rig it was measured on.

AutoEq profile (any headphone)

Make a profile for your headphone and target on autoeq.app, then load or paste ParametricEQ.txt, FixedBandEQ.txt, GraphicEQ.txt or the results CSV. It is fitted to the MixAmp's 14 bands.

Target curve

Add a target that isn't in the library, such as an IEF curve you're allowed to use (CSV, or squig.link / REW text). Pick the rig it was measured on. It's added under Imported and fitted to your headphone with AutoEq's method.
